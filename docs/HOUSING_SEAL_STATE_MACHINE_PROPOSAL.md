# Nauticase housing and seal state machine

**Approval draft · 22 November 2026 · WFH07 / A4.0**  
This document covers housing connection, power gaps, seal status, and pump protection. The approval decision covers both wording and button effects. An Android build and a housing-side safety qualification are separate deliverables.

## 1. Feasibility against the supplied specifications

Page numbers below count the PDF cover as page 1.

1. **The app can observe pressure, cap position and buttons.** WFH07 A4.0, PDF pp. 10–12, Table 7 and Table 8, defines external absolute pressure (0x1625), internal absolute air pressure (0x1627), suction-cover position (0x1628, 0 open, 1 closed), and button notifications. Depth needs an atmospheric reference. Vacuum needs a trustworthy surface reference for internal pressure. A cap-open byte establishes cap position only. Pressure can indicate a risk of ingress. It cannot identify liquid, salt, or a dry pump port.
2. **The app can command the motor and valve.** Motor 0x1624 and valve 0x1629 are write-only. The seven-step vendor procedure on PDF p. 5 opens the cap, opens the valve, starts the motor, stops it at target, confirms cap closure, closes the valve, then observes pressure for **more than five minutes**. The PDF specifies no numeric pump target or safe restart tolerance. An accepted write, a pressure drop and a recorded button press each fall short of proving the motor physically ran underwater.
3. **A4.0 does not establish the absolute pump guarantee.** The supplied interface documents neither a wet-port detector, firmware interlock, boot counter, motor-running feedback, retained pump incident, nor autonomous motor stop on BLE loss. The component interrupt pins are internal to the housing. There is no documented BLE access to such an interlock. A phone cannot observe or stop local operation while disconnected. Obtain a manufacturer statement for the specific hardware/firmware and bench-test the actual interlock before enabling a product mode that promises physical prevention. The alternative of accepting a depth-only pump guard would change the requested guarantee and requires a separate product decision.
4. **Reconnect works while the housing advertises.** A4.0 PDF p. 7 specifies a 200 ms advertising interval and a 180 s advertising duration. PDF p. 10 assigns power-on to Shutter and power-off to long OK. A BLE dropout and subsequent reconnect establish two link events. They give neither a definitive shutdown reason nor a history of locations between them. The shutter-hold wording in the app is an existing product instruction. Confirm timing on the target housing.
5. **Qualify near-surface and seal thresholds on the assembled unit.** The housing summary, PDF p. 6, lists 50 kPa external and 5 kPa internal resolution. The HP5834-10BA datasheet, p. 3, lists 0.01 kPa external resolution and up to ±2.5 kPa absolute error at 0–40 °C. The HP203N datasheet, p. 2, lists 0.01 mbar internal resolution. The project records finer BLE readings than the housing summary. Packet units, effective resolution, accuracy and wet-port detection are different properties. Existing dive thresholds and the 0.5 m release-prompt threshold are unsuitable as proof that the port is dry.

## 2. State list and ownership

Use one core resolver for housing safety. Feed it connection events, validated samples, user input, clock expiry and command outcomes. Both touch and housing controls consume the same result. Only approved banner IDs in §3 may be selected by this replacement. IDs, state names, diagnostics and reasons never appear as UI text. Use the existing banner surface and seal indicator. Existing higher-priority dive alerts keep their current display and input ownership.

1. **Disconnected.** On the first real loss, atomically save one pending record per housing before clearing live readings. Include gap ID, session, latest valid depth and vacuum with original sample times and quality, baseline, prior verified hold, suspected or known shutdown cause, pending motor command and unresolved incidents. A stale last sample is retained as history with its age. Invalidate start eligibility, cancel setup timers and queued starts, enter low-power reconnect, and display `LINK_LOST`. On initial discovery use `LINK_IDLE`. Record UTC, format exported time as YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM-SS, and use monotonic time within the running session for freshness and timers.
2. **Connecting and evaluating.** Show `LINK_CONNECTING` during service discovery and initial acquisition. Re-establish notifications and read fresh values from this housing and this connection. Start a bounded acquisition window using the existing pressure freshness policy. On expiry use the relevant sensor fault below. Restore pending state after app restart. A second dropout during evaluation keeps the earlier pending record and hazards. Never credit an unobserved interval as seal-test time.
3. **Current location.** Classify fresh external pressure against a validated local surface baseline, including sample age, uncertainty and hysteresis. Use surface, underwater or unknown. Loss of a valid baseline, contradictory values or borderline readings yield unknown. Process this on every new observation and on expiry even while continuously connected. Two surface endpoints do not establish that an intervening dive never occurred. A cold start with unknown previous depth goes through current-location rules, without inventing a previous endpoint.
4. **Current vacuum.** Normalize magnitude as trusted surface ambient pressure minus internal pressure. Do not subtract internal pressure from underwater external pressure. Classify absent, present below target, adequate and stable, or draining using qualified thresholds and multiple fresh samples. A lower endpoint establishes a difference only. An ongoing drain needs a trend. Use the prior verified hold and its current supporting evidence for verified status. Two matching endpoints cannot verify an unseen hold. Invalid current data, invalid history, absent history and a measured zero have distinct representations. If depth change, temperature, elapsed time or baseline change makes values incomparable, use the unverified branch.
5. **Resolve and persist.** Within the housing banner, a suspected or confirmed pump incident takes priority, followed by suspected ingress, unavailable current data, other degraded/unverified seal outcomes, setup, and normal operation. Use location-neutral wording when current location is unknown. Keep the link banner for ordinary link loss. If a pump or ingress incident is already active it keeps priority through loss and reconnect. Commit the chosen outcome and outstanding hazards with the gap ID before consuming the snapshot. Acknowledgement is per incident and presentation. Worsening severity, a fresh incident, or a transition to surface cleanup re-arms the appropriate banner.
6. **Pump permission.** Default motor and valve opening to denied. For the original physical prevention requirement, enable surface pumping only on a qualified housing/firmware combination that independently enforces protection at the exposed port, including power-up, local actions, shallow immersion, continued running, and link loss. If protection is enforced locally without a BLE dry flag, use the manufacturer's qualified behavior. Do not invent a BLE field. Also require a deliberate short Menu/OK on the visible start prompt, a trusted current link, fresh surface classification, valid internal pressure and fresh open-cap evidence. Recheck before valve-open and again inside the serialized motor-start write. Require successful valve-open before motor-start. Invalidate queued work on session or eligibility change. A timer, reconnect, pressure delta, acknowledgement, Up, or Down never starts a pump.
7. **Pumping and holding.** Reuse the existing vendor sequence, watchdog, no-suction/stall checks, and hold display. Persist “motor may be running” before sending start. On immersion, expired evidence, unexpected cap closure, interruption, or an uncertain write, cancel pending starts and request motor-off and valve-close as soon as the transport permits. Preserve the pending incident through process death, retry the stop/close on reconnect before any start, and record write outcomes. Do not report physical stop based on a timeout or an unsuccessful write. Confirm closed cap and observe a continuous valid hold for more than five minutes before a new vendor-qualified seal verdict. A pressure-only adoption, a three-minute provisional tier or silence across a gap cannot earn that verdict.
8. **Monitoring and recovery.** A healthy verified seal enables the camera with no new banner. An acknowledged caution returns button ownership to the camera and retains the existing seal status. An underwater instruction never asks the diver to open the housing or remove the cap. A suspected wet-pump or ingress incident carries forward to the dry-place inspection instruction after surfacing. Acknowledging a warning cannot clear the incident. For suspected ingress, the separate surface acknowledgement after inspection permits a new qualified seal test. Clear the incident only following that test. A suspected wet pump also requires manufacturer-approved service/recovery before powered testing. A4.0 exposes no service-clear command. Keep powered restart inhibited pending an approved recovery method.

The line breaks shown with `<br>` are display line breaks. **NONE** renders no housing banner. Automatic text wrapping is allowed. No new button, dialogue, badge, notification, vibration or sound is part of this proposal. The catalog applies to this resolver and its recovery paths. Do not interpolate raw transport errors or select an older seal banner underneath an active warning.

On successful target and motor-off command, reuse **VACUUM REACHED** / **CLOSE THE BLUE CAP** / **Press Menu/OK for camera controls** on the surface. After fresh cap closure show the existing hold indicator. For dry, eligible starts, retain the existing exact **NO VACUUM SUCTION DETECTED** and **VACUUM NOT BUILDING** copy from `SealCheckIndicator.kt` only while their retry instructions are valid. Re-evaluate before accepting any retry. Suppress legacy release/restart and failure prompts wherever this resolver owns the result. Short Up in the existing failure UI resets the check. Short Down in the onboarding cap tutorial advances the tutorial. Neither is a pump-start instruction.

## 4. All 28 reconnect outcomes

The first letter identifies **last reliable location → current location**. The second identifies **historical → current vacuum magnitude**. Here “present” means measurable, not verified. Apply incidents, freshness and validity first. A current reading that is inadequate or draining cannot select NONE. Where surface setup is specified, choose SETUP_UNAVAILABLE for unqualified hardware, CAP_UNKNOWN for unknown cap position on qualified hardware, CAP for closed cap or START for open cap. With uncertain location show DEPTH_UNKNOWN. Apply this selection before showing INSERT or any instruction to remove the cap.

### A. Surface → surface

1. **A–A · Present → absent.** Known intentional release in dry setup context → INSERT, then surface setup. Unexplained loss → SURFACE_WEAK. Evidence of possible ingress → INSPECT.
2. **A–B · Present → lower present.** Comparable, significant weakening → SURFACE_WEAK.
3. **A–C · Present → same or higher.** Healthy verified hold → NONE. Inadequate → SURFACE_LOW. Adequate but unverified → HOLD_UNVERIFIED.
4. **A–E · Absent → absent.** Surface setup.
5. **A–F · Absent → present.** Healthy verified current hold → NONE. Otherwise apply current weak/low/unverified rules. No warning solely because the old measurement was zero.
6. **A–G · No history → absent.** Surface setup.
7. **A–H · No history → present.** Same current-seal decision as A–F. A missing snapshot cannot establish a prior failure.

### B. Underwater → underwater

8. **B–A · Present → absent.** Reliable evidence of complete vacuum loss → WATER_LOSS.
9. **B–B · Present → lower present.** Comparable, significant weakening → WATER_WEAK. Incomparable readings → WATER_UNVERIFIED.
10. **B–C · Present → same or higher.** Healthy verified hold with supporting current evidence → NONE. Otherwise WATER_WEAK for measured weakness, WATER_UNVERIFIED for uncertain evidence.
11. **B–E · Absent → absent.** WATER_ABSENT.
12. **B–F · Absent → present.** WATER_UNVERIFIED. A pressure gain alone never selects PUMP_CONFIRMED.
13. **B–G · No history → absent.** WATER_ABSENT.
14. **B–H · No history → present.** WATER_VACUUM. PUMP_POSSIBLE or PUMP_CONFIRMED overrides only with the independent evidence in §3.

### C. Surface → underwater

15. **C–A · Present → absent.** Reliable evidence of complete vacuum loss → WATER_LOSS.
16. **C–B · Present → lower present.** Comparable, significant weakening → WATER_WEAK. Incomparable readings → WATER_UNVERIFIED.
17. **C–C · Present → same or higher.** Same verified, weak or unverified outcome as B–C.
18. **C–E · Absent → absent.** WATER_ABSENT.
19. **C–F · Absent → present.** WATER_UNVERIFIED. The vacuum could have been established before entering the water.
20. **C–G · No history → absent.** WATER_ABSENT. Carry the inspection obligation into the same surface warning as D–G.
21. **C–H · No history → present.** WATER_VACUUM. Apply the same incident override as B–H.

### D. Underwater → surface

22. **D–A · Present → absent.** INSPECT. A dive preceded an unexplained complete loss. No automatic restoration.
23. **D–B · Present → lower present.** Comparable, significant weakening → INSPECT. If comparison is inconclusive and no stronger incident exists → POST_DIVE.
24. **D–C · Present → same or higher.** Healthy verified hold with supporting current evidence → NONE. Inconclusive → POST_DIVE. Current low/draining vacuum after the dive → INSPECT.
25. **D–E · Absent → absent.** INSPECT.
26. **D–F · Absent → present.** POST_DIVE.
27. **D–G · No history → absent.** INSPECT, identical to D–E.
28. **D–H · No history → present.** POST_DIVE. The time of establishment and the previous dive seal are unknown.

## 5. Continuous operation, exceptions and acceptance

**Selection order within valid telemetry.** Apply independent pump or ingress incidents first. For a physically comparable transition to measured absence, use the appropriate loss/absence row. For present but draining vacuum, use SURFACE_WEAK or WATER_WEAK, or INSPECT for unresolved weakening after a dive. For present but stable below target use SURFACE_LOW or WATER_WEAK, or INSPECT following a dive with suspected loss. For the remaining present, adequate readings apply the numbered row. A newly measured vacuum with uncertain history underwater keeps its history warning. When the count of valid current samples is insufficient to judge a trend, keep the outcome unverified until the trend can be assessed. Qualify the uncertainty bands, sample windows and hysteresis on the target hardware before enabling decisions that depend on them. During low-power reconnect reduce camera/render and scan work where appropriate, preserving active dive context and unresolved warnings.

1. **No disconnect.** Run incident and current-seal rules on notifications, reads and timeouts. Underwater, complete loss after established vacuum → WATER_LOSS, a known low or weakening vacuum → WATER_WEAK, measured absence with no prior established vacuum → WATER_ABSENT, and insufficient verification → WATER_UNVERIFIED. At the surface, a newly draining seal → SURFACE_WEAK, a stable below-target seal → SURFACE_LOW, and previously underwater unresolved concerns → INSPECT for suspected ingress/pump activity or POST_DIVE for uncertain previous seal. A healthy verified hold → NONE. Surfacing while power remains on triggers the same cleanup instruction as a reconnect.
2. **Unobserved transitions.** Surface on → underwater off, surface off → underwater on, off at both endpoints and on at both endpoints are valid physical sequences. Only classify measured endpoints. Apply the same rule on ascent. While both endpoints are off the phone can keep a snapshot and scan. It has no live housing observation.
3. **Missing inputs.** Without a stronger incident, unknown current depth → DEPTH_UNKNOWN, then unknown current vacuum → VACUUM_UNKNOWN. Invalid or noncomparable stored pressure with valid current data → use current-location degraded/unverified rules. Treat an invalid history as untrusted. Missing vacuum history with known previous location can use G/H. Missing previous location cannot produce a two-letter combination. At first contact underwater, measured absence → WATER_ABSENT, weak → WATER_WEAK, otherwise unverified → WATER_UNVERIFIED. First contact at surface with measured absence → surface setup. No normal verdict on stale telemetry.
4. **Approval scope.** E denotes existing words, S the supplied specification, and P requires copy approval. All new mappings require approval, including stronger warnings for measured absence underwater, inspection for D–A/D–B, qualified-only normal operation, the use of short Menu/OK, and the proposed unsupported-hardware and recovery behavior. Copy remains exactly as catalogued once approved. The resolver must never manufacture a banner from debug text or combine two approved banners into a new message.
5. **Programmer acceptance.** Parameterize tests for every numbered combination and assert the exact banner ID, exact rendered strings, input owner, persisted incident and emitted commands. Test faults and incident overrides against every location, including stale, invalid and missing values, noncomparable baselines, interrupted five-minute holds, repeated BLE flaps, app restarts, changing housings, queued starts during immersion, failed valve-open, uncertain stop writes and a running pump at disconnect. Verify warning acknowledgement cannot start a pump or clear seal status. Test unknown-cap/unknown-depth conditions during tutorial, retries and shutdown/restart choices as well as the main start prompt. Verify the 4.5-second timer, consumed early button, one-time snapshot evaluation, camera usability, low-power reconnect, and transition to cleanup on surfacing.
6. **Hardware acceptance.** On an empty instrumented housing, measure actual motor current, pressure, cap events and valve behavior independently of BLE. Verify cold boot in water, local buttons, loss of signal or app process during a run, sensor fault, stale sensor data, retained water at a surface port and lowering the port through the waterline. Include nominal dry setup and post-dive recovery. Use a protected fixture or simulated wet input before any test that could admit water to the pump. Record housing revision, firmware, effective sample resolution, tolerated changes across depth/temperature, and protection response times. An APK or passing Android tests cannot certify these physical results.
